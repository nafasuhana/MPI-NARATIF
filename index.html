Materi: Memahami Teks Naratif

Read, understand, and explore the story.

1. Definition

Narrative text is a text that tells a story or a sequence of events. It is usually written to entertain readers and often conveys a moral lesson.

2. Social Function

To entertain readers, engage their imagination, and communicate values or lessons through a story.

3. Generic Structure

Orientation

Introduces the characters, setting, and time.

Complication

Presents the problem or conflict.

Resolution

Explains how the problem is solved.

Reorientation / Coda

Closing comment or moral lesson (optional).

4. Language Features

  • Simple Past Tense: studied, became, helped.
  • Action Verbs: walked, supported, prepared.
  • Time Connectives: one day, then, finally.
  • Adjectives: friendly, hardworking, sincere.
  • Direct Speech: “I will always support you.”

The Story of a Friendly Future

An original narrative for English learning

Orientation

In the year 2040, there were two students named Alex and Ben. They studied at a modern vocational school. Alex was a hardworking student, while Ben was friendly and helpful. They became good friends because they always supported each other.

Complication

One day, Alex became successful in a school technology competition. Many students wanted to be his friends because he received a valuable prize and became popular. Some students only wanted to use his money and reputation. Ben also faced a difficult situation when his family experienced financial problems.

Alex began to wonder whether his new friends really cared about him. He realized that some friendships were based on money and social status rather than honesty and kindness.

Resolution

Ben continued to support Alex even when he had nothing to offer. He helped Alex prepare for another competition and never asked for anything in return. Alex finally understood that true friendship was not about money, power, or position. It was about trust, respect, and helping one another.

Moral Lesson

True friendship comes from sincerity, honesty, loyalty, and mutual respect. We should choose friends who appreciate us for who we are, not for what we have.

5. Vocabulary Corner

Hardworking = rajin bekerja/tekun
Reputation = reputasi/nama baik
Sincere = tulus
Support = mendukung
Position = jabatan/kedudukan
Mutual respect = saling menghormati
